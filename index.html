Renovation Builders Brisbane Budget Tips Explained

When comparing renovation builders in Brisbane, understanding the distinction between small upgrades and major structural work is crucial. A simple kitchen refresh, a bathroom redesign, a house extension, or a complete home renovation each have different demands regarding budget, necessary approvals, documentation, and the builder's capacity. Practical starting budget ranges provide a useful benchmark for assessing project scope.

Kitchen renovations are commonly quoted between $25,000 and $60,000+, while bathroom renovations typically fall between $22,000 and $35,000+. For larger projects, house extensions generally start from $150,000, and full home renovations can range from $150,000 up to $500,000 or more. These figures serve as indicators of scope rather than fixed prices. Homeowners should always clarify what is included and excluded, whether fixtures are allowance items, and if demolition, electrical, plumbing, painting, cabinetry, and flooring are covered within the same written quote.

Licence, Membership, and Scope Checks

Builders undertaking renovation work in Brisbane are expected to hold a QBCC licence. Membership with industry bodies like the Housing Industry Association (HIA) or Master Builders Queensland is also referenced. For homeowners, the critical step is to verify that the builder's licence category is appropriate for the specific work being quoted. Cosmetic updates for a single room differ significantly from structural alterations, extensions, or heritage-sensitive renovations. It is advisable to request the builder's QBCC licence details before agreeing to any quote. Clarify whether structural work, plumbing, electrical services, and necessary certifications are included or managed separately. Understand the process for pricing and approving variations before work commences, and confirm who is responsible for communications with council, certifiers, or town planners regarding required approvals.

Approvals Need Early Attention

Council approval is generally required for renovations that alter a building's footprint or structure. Internal renovations that do not change the building's external form typically do not need planning approval. However, a building permit is necessary for structural alterations, extensions, and significant layout changes. Properties within heritage overlay zones will require additional specific approvals. Therefore, approvals should be addressed early in the planning process, not as an afterthought. Homeowners should clearly describe whether walls will be moved, new rooms added, the building envelope modified, or if heritage constraints apply. Council approval timelines can range from 2 to 8 weeks, depending on the project's complexity, potentially impacting the start date and overall project sequencing.

How To Read and Request a Quote

A comprehensive renovation quote should enable homeowners to compare project scopes directly, not just the final figures. If one quote includes services like design documentation, demolition, fixture supply, and painting, while another lists these as exclusions, the lower price may not reflect the true overall cost. The process for requesting quotes is generally straightforward, with many builders providing obligation-free estimates within 48 hours. To ensure accurate comparisons, the initial brief should be detailed enough for builders to price the same scope of work. Useful information to include in a quote request comprises room dimensions, desired finishes, photographs of the existing space, details of any known structural changes, site access limitations, and concerns regarding approvals. For kitchen and bathroom projects, specify how cabinetry, waterproofing, plumbing, electrical work, and fixture allowances are to be handled. For extensions or full renovations, request a staged breakdown covering design, documentation, pre-construction, and construction phases.

  1. Define the Scope. Write down the specific rooms, structural changes, desired finishes, and must-have outcomes before requesting quotes.
  2. Check Licence Fit. Confirm the builder's QBCC licence details and ensure the licence category is suitable for the proposed work.
  3. Raise Approvals Early. Discuss footprint, structural, and heritage constraints upfront to clarify timing and responsibilities.
  4. Compare Inclusions. Review quotes against the same scope, allowances, exclusions, and variation processes, not just total costs.
